Why Low-Frequency Lineup Scrambles Bleed Points Before They Ever Stabilize

1 · The League-Wide Sample (All 31 Teams)

Standard lineup tables conveniently truncate small-sample units, letting staffs treat sporadic substitutions as harmless noise. Across 149,952 uncapped G League combinations, low-rep units bleed points at a statistically undeniable clip (p < 0.0001) until clearing their per-game stabilization floor:

5-Man <1.5 poss/g: -10.7 NetRtg Quads <3.5 poss/g: -9.8 NetRtg Trios <6.5 poss/g: -11.6 NetRtg Duos <7.5 poss/g: -19.4 NetRtg

Pontificating about rotation chemistry in the abstract misses the mechanism. Every unpracticed pairing starts in an immediate execution deficit and burns several minutes per game just to reach break-even. When a staff treats bench substitutions like a random number generator (tossing cold five-man units together for three-possession cameos), it spots the opponent free runs before the core rotation gets back on the floor.
